Privacy Policy for Event Auto

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Event Auto ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data when you use our application at auto-event.github.io.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google OAuth, we may collect:

  • Basic profile information: name, email address, profile picture (from Google OAuth scopes: openid, email, profile)

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate your identity and provide access to Event Auto features.

  • We do NOT sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is processed securely. OAuth tokens are stored securely and are not shared. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information. You can revoke our access at any time via Google Account Permissions.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide the service or until you request deletion. If you delete your account or revoke access, we will delete your associated data within 30 days.

We use Google OAuth 2.0 and Google Cloud Platform for authentication. Their privacy policies also apply: Google Privacy Policy.
